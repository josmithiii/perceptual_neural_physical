EfficientNet-B0 is the base model of the EfficientNet family, designed using neural architecture search with a compound scaling method. Here's its architecture:

Key Characteristics
Input: 224×224×3 images (though your code modifies this via the conv2d layer before it)
Total parameters: ~5.3 million
Depth: 18 main blocks (plus stem and head)
Architecture Structure

1. Stem:
   - Conv2d 3×3, stride 2, 32 filters
   - BatchNorm + Swish activation

2. MBConv Blocks (Mobile Inverted Bottleneck Convolution):
    Stage 1: 1 block, 16 filters, 1×1 kernel, stride 1
    Stage 2: 2 blocks, 24 filters, 3×3 kernel, stride 2
    Stage 3: 2 blocks, 40 filters, 5×5 kernel, stride 2
    Stage 4: 3 blocks, 80 filters, 3×3 kernel, stride 2
    Stage 5: 3 blocks, 112 filters, 5×5 kernel, stride 1
    Stage 6: 4 blocks, 192 filters, 5×5 kernel, stride 2
    Stage 7: 1 block, 320 filters, 3×3 kernel, stride 1

3. Head:
    Conv2d 1×1, 1280 filters
    Global average pooling
    Fully connected layer → num_classes (in your case, outdim)

Key Features

    Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE): Attention mechanism in each MBConv block
    Swish activation: x * sigmoid(x) instead of ReLU
    Depthwise separable convolutions: Reduces parameters and computation
    Compound scaling: B1-B7 scale width, depth, and resolution proportionally

In your code: The num_classes=outdim replaces the final FC layer to
output your desired parameter dimensions instead of ImageNet's 1000 classes.

⏺ [0,1] vs [-1,1] Normalization: Should You Change?

  Short Answer: No fundamental difference - keep [0,1] if it's working!

  Here's the analysis:

  Mathematical Equivalence

  Both normalizations are affine transformations of the same data:

  [0,1] scaling:
  ν₀₁ = (θ - θ_min) / (θ_max - θ_min)
  range = 1

  [-1,1] scaling:
  ν₋₁₁ = 2·(θ - θ_min)/(θ_max - θ_min) - 1 = 2·ν₀₁ - 1
  range = 2

  Conversion:
  ν₋₁₁ = 2·ν₀₁ - 1
  ν₀₁ = (ν₋₁₁ + 1)/2

  Impact on Training

  | Aspect                   | [0,1]              | [-1,1]             | Winner        |
  |--------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|---------------|
  | Gradient flow            | Same               | Same               | Tie           |
  | Optimization landscape   | Identical shape    | Identical shape    | Tie           |
  | Parameter initialization | Slightly different | Slightly different | Tie           |
  | Activation function      | Sigmoid natural    | Tanh natural       | Depends       |
  | MSE interpretation       | MSE ≤ 1            | MSE ≤ 4            | [0,1] cleaner |

  Key Differences

  1. Final Activation Layer

  Looking at your code (cnn.py:89):
